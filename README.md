## Abstract

**The Cybernetic Atlas** is an open research repository synthesizing systems theory, behavioral cybernetics, and protocol engineering into unified, interactive models of autonomous command.

Human and synthetic interaction networks frequently exhibit pathological failure modes: status games that destroy communicative bandwidth, emotional thermostats that force compliance over reality, and capacitive overload in high-variance nervous systems. The Atlas formalizes these dynamics into three standalone interactive treatises and physics-based visual simulators.

## The Three Core Treatises

### 1. The Architecture of the Herd (Sheep OS)
*Homeostatic Social Regulation, Consensus Loops, and Defensive Pack Firewalls.*
- **Focus:** Why consensus-seeking socio-biological networks lack an internal semantic compiler, operating instead as analog radios tuned to ambient threat.
- **Key Concepts:** The Homeostatic Thermostat, Scoreboard Games, Moral Hegemony Traps, The 4-Phase Parasite Lifecycle, and Naturalist Detachment.
- **Access:** [Read Book](books/sheep-os/reader.html) · [Launch Interactive Atlas](books/sheep-os/interactive_atlas.html)

### 2. The Sovereign Engine
*Hardware Governance, Energy Thermodynamics, and Ontological Sovereignty for High-Variance Cognitive Substrates.*
- **Focus:** Decoupling executive command authority from performative approval loops.
- **Key Concepts:** The 4 Thermodynamic Fires, The Animal Layer vs Shadow distinction, Sensory Entropy Containment, Cognitive Load Classes, and The Great Refusal.
- **Access:** [Read Book](books/sovereign-engine/reader.html) · [Launch Interactive Atlas](books/sovereign-engine/interactive_atlas.html)

### 3. The Frame Stack
*An OSI Telecommunications Model for Human & Agentic Encounters.*
- **Focus:** Treating multi-agent communication as a strictly layered telecommunications stack where transport stability strictly gates semantic transmission.
- **Key Concepts:** The 7 Layers of Encounter, Low-Baud vs High-Baud Carrier Rates, Emotional Capacitive Circuits, The Master Engagement Equation, and Deficit-Counter Diagnostics.
- **Access:** [Read Book](books/frame-stack/reader.html) · [Launch Interactive Atlas](books/frame-stack/interactive_atlas.html)
